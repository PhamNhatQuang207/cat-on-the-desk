"Cat on a Desk (Don't Touch Anything!)"
The Premise: You play as a cat whose sole purpose in life is to knock expensive/fragile items off a desk while your owner tries to stop you.

Gameplay:

First-person or side-scroller view of a desk loaded with mugs, laptops, glasses, and houseplants.

Use paw swipes to slowly push items toward the edge.

Establish direct eye contact with the human AI while pushing an item down to gain bonus points.

Dodge spray bottles and reaching hands.

Why It’s Funny: Taps into classic cat chaotic-evil energy.
